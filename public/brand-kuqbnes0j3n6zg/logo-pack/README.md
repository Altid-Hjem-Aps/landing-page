# Altid Hjem logo pack

November 2026. Every Altid Hjem logo and icon in the formats you need, with the rules from the Altid Hjem brand book. Questions: hej@altidhjem.dk

## What is in the pack

| Folder | What | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| `wordmark/` | The Altid Hjem wordmark in Deep forest and white. SVG, plus PNG at 480, 960 and 1920 px wide, transparent background | Deep forest on Cream or light backgrounds. White on Deep forest or photos. |
| `app-icon/` | The app icon. SVG with square corners and with rounded corners. PNG at 1024, 512, 256, 128, 64 and 48 px in both versions | Square (`-1024.png` etc.): app stores and anywhere the platform rounds the corners itself; these PNGs are opaque on purpose, as the stores require. Rounded (`-rounded-N.png`): everywhere else, such as slides, web pages and partner sites |
| `lockup/` | App icon and wordmark side by side, as in the mail header. SVG, plus PNG at 960 and 1920 px wide | Headers and partner pages where both the icon and the name should show. Deep forest on light backgrounds, white on Deep forest |
| `service-icons/` | The six service icons from the app: madplan, strom, forsikring, opladning, mobil, alarm. Each as a Deep forest drawing (`name.svg`) and in a pastel circle with a white ring (`name-circle.svg`) | Use the circle version in pictures, as the brand book does. Use the plain drawing only on a pastel or Cream surface |
| `sub-brands/` | Altid Energi logos, unchanged from the app: full colour, navy with white, inverted, all white | Only for Altid Energi. Altid Energi has its own colours (navy #033B8C, light blue #73D0E7) |
| `colours/` | `colours.json` and `colours.css` (CSS custom properties) with the eleven brand colours | Design tools and code |

Altid Mad and Altid Forsikring have no approved logos of their own yet. Use the Altid Hjem wordmark or lockup for them.

## Clear space and minimum size

- **Wordmark clear space:** keep a free space of x on every side, where x is the height of the a in "altid" (about half the wordmark's height).
- **App icon clear space:** at least a quarter of the icon's width on every side.
- **Lockup clear space:** x on every side, measured on the wordmark part.
- **Minimum size:** the wordmark at least 96 px wide on screen or 25 mm in print. The app icon at least 24 px. The lockup at least 160 px wide, so the wordmark inside it stays above 96 px.

## Do and don't

Do:
- Place the wordmark on Cream, on Deep forest or on a pastel card.
- Keep the app icon square and on its own, with its rounded corners.

Never:
- Stretch or squash the logo.
- Recolour it. Use only the colours in this pack.
- Add a shadow.
- Use the word "altid" alone without "hjem".
- Cut the house out of the app icon's background.

## Good to know

- The fonts are DM Sans and Onest, both free on Google Fonts. They are not part of this pack.
- There is no white or one-colour version of the app icon. Use it as it is on every background.
